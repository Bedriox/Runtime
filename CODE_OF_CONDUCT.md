# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

The Bedriox community is committed to a respectful, welcoming, and productive
environment. Participate constructively, assume good faith while evaluating
technical evidence, and make room for people with different backgrounds and
experience levels.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate disruption,
and publication of private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method published by the
Bedriox organization. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, limit
participation, or take other proportionate action to protect the community.
Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
